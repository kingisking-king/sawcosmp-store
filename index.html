FAQ

Can't find your answer? Ask us on Discord and a staff member will help.

How do I join the server?

Open Minecraft Java Edition, go to Multiplayer → Add Server and paste . Hit the PLAY NOW button above to copy it.

How long does delivery take?

Most purchases arrive within a few minutes. Make sure you type your Minecraft username exactly right at checkout. If you're offline, items are delivered the next time you join.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Checkout is handled by Tebex, which supports cards, PayPal and many local payment methods. We never see your payment details.

Are ranks permanent?

Yes, ranks are one-time purchases and stay on your account unless you break the server rules.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are digital and generally non-refundable. If something went wrong with your order, reach out on Discord and we'll sort it out.

Is this pay-to-win?

No. Perks focus on convenience and quality-of-life, so survival stays fair for everyone. Crate keys can also be earned for free by voting.
